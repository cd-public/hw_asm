THE REGISTER FILE

Reminder: What is a register?

■ Registers: special locations built directly into the hardware to store data! ■ These serve as the operands of many assembly instructions ■ These primitives are also visible to the assembly programmer once the computer is built. ■ The size of a register is determined by the architecture – We are assuming 32-bit registers in this class! ■ Registers are also synchronized with a clock! – We will get to this next week. ☺ ■ The processor’s 32 general-purpose registers are stored in a structure called a register file
