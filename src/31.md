Minimizing Logic
■ Previously, we have discussed how to systematically convert logic
specified as a truth table into sum-of-products (SOP).
■ After that, we relied on cleverness, and luck to reduce these expressions
via the properties and theorems of Boolean algebra.
■ Today we will discuss a more systematic method for logic minimization,
called a Karnaugh map, that is based on the clustering of terms.
