# Transistors


## Physical Building Block

- A transistor is a physical device to turn continuous electrical behavior into digital `0`s and `1`s.
- An electronically controlled switch turns current *on* or *off*

## Transistors

- We will be working with two types of transistors
    - nMOS transistors
    - pMOS transistors
- There are three terminals
    - Gate: controls whether the transistor is on or off
    - Source and Drain: endpoints
